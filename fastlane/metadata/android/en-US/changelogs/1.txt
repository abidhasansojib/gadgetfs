## Changelog (v1.0)

* Initial release
* Root-based provisioning and activation of USB gadget roles via ConfigFS
* HID keyboard and mouse gadget setup (function creation, linking, UDC bind/unbind)
* Configurable gadget identity where supported (descriptors/strings)
* Status reporting for gadget readiness and UDC binding
* Safer teardown and cleanup to reduce partial/failed setup states
